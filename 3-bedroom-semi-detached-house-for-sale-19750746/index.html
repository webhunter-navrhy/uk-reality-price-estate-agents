About the property

Description property

Nestled in the desirable St Johns area of Worcester, this modern three bedroom semi detached house presents an exceptional opportunity for buyers seeking a modern family home. This property combines stylish interiors with practical features, making it an ideal choice for a variety of purchasers especially families.

Upon entering, you are greeted by a welcoming entrance hall that leads into a spacious lounge, providing an inviting area for relaxation and entertaining. The heart of this home is undoubtedly the modern fitted kitchen/dining room. This well-appointed space boasts contemporary units, ample work surfaces, and some integrated appliances.

The property benefits from three well-proportioned bedrooms with the master bedroom featuring its own en-suite shower room. A further modern bathroom serves the remaining bedrooms.

Comfort and efficiency are assured with gas central heating and double glazing throughout, contributing to a warm and energy-efficient home.

Occupying an enviable position overlooking a maintained green, there is convenient parking for two cars, and the rear of the property features an enclosed garden, offering a private outdoor space.

Located in St Johns, residents benefit from excellent local amenities, including shops, schools, and leisure facilities, all within easy reach. Worcester city centre or Malvern with its broader range of shopping, dining, and cultural attractions, is also just a short drive away. Excellent transport links provide easy access to surrounding areas and major road networks, making this an ideal location for commuters.

This modern semi-detached house offers a fantastic blend of style, comfort, and convenience in a highly sought-after Worcester location. Early viewing is highly recommended to fully appreciate all that this superb property has to offer.
